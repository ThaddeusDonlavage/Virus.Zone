Reality Hacker / Vector / Trickster

Hatters weaponize the Veil itself. They turn impossible logic into battlefield leverage, forcing the world to obey a new set of rules for a heartbeat or a scene. Turning impossible conditions into tactical advantages, there intrusion into reality leave witnesses unsure whether they saw a trick, an exploit or a miracle.
